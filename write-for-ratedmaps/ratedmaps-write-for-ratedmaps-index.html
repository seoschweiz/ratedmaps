Why write for RatedMaps?

RatedMaps is about discovering places through maps, recommendations and real local knowledge.

Contributors can introduce their expertise, project or local knowledge to new readers. Accepted articles may include an author name, short biography, website and relevant social media profile.

Submission Guidelines

✓ Articles should be original and useful to readers.

✓ We particularly value genuine local knowledge and personal experience.

✓ Recommended article length: 700–2,000 words.

✓ Relevant links are welcome when they genuinely add value.

✓ Pure advertising, spam and duplicated content will not be accepted.

✓ RatedMaps may edit submissions for formatting, clarity and quality.
